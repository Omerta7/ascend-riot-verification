ASCEND is a community-based competitive platform designed to help VALORANT players review match history, track performance, and participate in organized regional custom matchmaking.

OUR MISSION

About ASCEND

ASCEND aims to provide players with accessible match information and a structured competitive community. Our goal is to support player improvement, fairer team balancing, and a more organized matchmaking experience.

PLATFORM FEATURES

Built for Competitive Players

Match History

Planned integration with the VALORANT Match API to help consenting players review available match records and match details, subject to Riot approval.

Performance Tracking

Aims to help players understand their performance over time using available match statistics.

Regional Matchmaking

Community custom-match queues for Korea and Asia, with internal rating-based team balancing.

Reports & Moderation

A community reporting and staff review process designed to support a fair and organized environment.
